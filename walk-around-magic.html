Walk-Around Magic

Roaming, close-up magic performed table to table and group to group — built for mingling, conversation, and constant surprise.

The Experience

Magic Woven Into Your Event

Walk-around magic brings the performance directly to your guests. Instead of gathering everyone in one place, Gage moves naturally through cocktail hours, receptions, and mingling crowds — sitting down with small groups for a few minutes of astonishment before moving on to the next.

It's the ideal fit for corporate mixers, wedding cocktail hours, holiday parties, and private gatherings where guests are already up, talking, and socializing. No stage, no setup, no lull in the evening — just magic that fits seamlessly into the flow of your event.

Benefits

Why Hosts Choose Walk-Around Magic
