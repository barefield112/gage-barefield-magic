Wedding & Cocktail Hour Magic

I'll work close-up magic into your cocktail hour and reception, a fun way to keep guests entertained while you and your new spouse steal a quiet moment away.

The Experience

Magic That Feels Like Part of the Celebration

Cocktail hour is the perfect window for close-up magic: guests are mingling, drinks in hand, waiting on the reception to start. I move table to table, group to group, and turn that waiting time into one of the most talked-about parts of the day.

It slots right into your existing timeline: no stage, no sound system, and no disruption to your photographer's schedule. Just genuine, jaw-dropping moments woven into the celebration.
