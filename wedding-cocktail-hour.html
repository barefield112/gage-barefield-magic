Wedding & Cocktail Hour Magic

I'll work close-up magic into your cocktail hour and reception, so your guests stay entertained while you two sneak away for a quiet moment.

The Experience

How It Works

Cocktail hour is the perfect time for close-up magic. Guests are mingling with drinks in hand, waiting on the reception to start. I move table to table and turn that waiting time into one of the most talked-about parts of the day.

It fits right into your timeline with no stage, no sound system, and no getting in your photographer's way. Just fun, up-close moments mixed into the celebration.
