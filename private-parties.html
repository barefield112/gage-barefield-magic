How It Works

Private parties are honestly where magic shines the most: birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and holiday gatherings all get a little more special with close-up magic that pulls family and friends into the moment together.

I tailor what I do to the room I'm in, whether that's a living room full of kids at a birthday party or a mixed-generation family gathering, either way, everyone gets a moment to remember.
