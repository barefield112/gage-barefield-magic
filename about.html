The Story

Magic Rooted in Connection

Gage Barefield is a Knoxville-area magician specializing in close-up and stage magic for corporate events, weddings, and private parties. What started as a personal fascination with sleight of hand has grown into a career built on one simple idea: the best magic happens when it's shared, up close, with real reactions.

Every performance is designed around the room — reading the crowd, adjusting the pace, and making sure the people in front of him feel like part of the moment rather than spectators to it. Whether it's a boardroom, a reception hall, or a living room, the goal is the same: leave people talking about it long after the night ends.

Gage has performed for corporate mixers, wedding cocktail hours, holiday parties, and private celebrations throughout the Knoxville area, bringing the same level of polish and care to every booking, large or small.
